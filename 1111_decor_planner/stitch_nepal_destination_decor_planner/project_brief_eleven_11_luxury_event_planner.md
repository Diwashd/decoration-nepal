# Project Brief: Eleven 11 - Luxury Event Planner

## Project Overview
**Client:** Eleven 11 Decoration Nepal  
**Goal:** To redesign and elevate the digital presence of Eleven 11, transforming it from a traditional service provider into Nepal's premier luxury event planning and destination experience platform.

---

## Brand Identity & Aesthetic
The project is anchored by the **"Aura of Eleven"** design system.
- **Visual Direction:** Opulent, sophisticated, and celebratory.
- **Color Palette:** Deep Charcoal (#131313) surfaces with Metallic Gold (#D4AF37) accents, reflecting the brand's logo and high-end positioning.
- **Typography:** **Playfair Display** for headings to convey elegance; **Inter** (or similar sans-serif) for functional UI elements to ensure clarity.
- **Imagery:** High-fidelity, atmospheric photography of Nepalese landscapes, intricate mandap designs, and intimate celebration moments.

---

## Core Product Pillars

### 1. Destination-First Exploration
Unlike traditional planners, Eleven 11 positions itself as a destination expert. 
- **Features:** A curated "Destinations Explorer" showcasing key regions (Kathmandu Valley, Pokhara, Chitwan). 
- **User Value:** Helps clients visualize their events in specific cultural or natural contexts, paired with local venue recommendations.

### 2. Specialized Decoration Catalog
Showcasing the depth of NK's decoration mastery.
- **Segments:** Wedding Mandaps, Experience Services (Surprise Plans, Proposals), and Milestone Celebrations (Birthdays, Pasni).
- **Transparency:** Clear "Starting From" pricing and high-impact visual themes like "Royal Heritage" and "Modern Minimalist."

### 3. Curated Event Orchestration
A streamlined, white-glove inquiry process.
- **Interactive Planner:** A multi-step form ("Basics," "Destination," "Theme," "Details") that builds an "Estimate Profile" in real-time.
- **Personalization:** Tailors the baseline quote to the user's specific vision before the first consultation.

---

## Key Screens & Features

### Home Page: The Entry to Excellence
- **Hero:** Immersive video/image background with a quick "Plan Now" widget.
- **Bespoke Services:** Icon-based navigation for Decoration, Venue Finding, Photo & Video, and Makeup.
- **Social Proof:** "Voices of Joy" testimonial section featuring high-profile client feedback.

### Destinations Page: Celebrate Across Nepal
- **Filtering:** Refine by Region (Lalitpur, Nagarkot) and Venue Type (Resort, Garden, Banquet).
- **Thematic Pairings:** Suggesting specific decoration styles that fit the local environment (e.g., "Organic Elegance" for Pokhara).

### Services Page: The Catalog of Masterpieces
- **Gallery-led UI:** Cards that highlight the unique architectural elegance of each setup.
- **Action-oriented:** Direct booking or inquiry CTAs for specific packages.

---

## Technical Considerations
- **Device Support:** Desktop-first (current focus) with full mobile responsiveness planned.
- **Performance:** Optimization of high-resolution image assets to ensure a premium browsing experience without lag.
- **Accessibility:** Ensuring high contrast for gold-on-charcoal text elements to maintain readability.

---

## Future Roadmap
- **Portfolio Gallery:** A dedicated, filterable gallery of past "Masterpieces."
- **Vendor Portal:** Integration with venue partners for real-time availability.
- **Client Dashboard:** A secure portal for booked clients to track planning milestones.
